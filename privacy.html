Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026  ·  Last Updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nate Templeton ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information in connection with The Accountability Challenge Co. (TAC Co.) website, theaccountabilityco.com.

What This Site Is

theaccountabilityco.com is a marketing hub for three separate 90-day accountability challenges: SMAC (joinsmac.com), WAC (joinwac.com), and AAC (jointheaac.com). This site does not process payments, host a member platform, or collect proof submissions directly — clicking a challenge card takes you to that challenge's own website, which has its own privacy and cookie policies.

Information We Collect

We collect information automatically through the Meta Pixel and standard browser technologies when you visit this site. See the Cookies & Tracking Technologies section below for details. We do not collect names, emails, or payment information on this site itself.

How We Use Your Information

  • To measure and improve the performance of our ads, and to build audiences for future advertising
  • To understand which challenges visitors are most interested in
  • To improve this site going forward

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

This site uses the Meta Pixel, a tracking tool from Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook/Instagram), to measure ad performance and understand how visitors use our site. The pixel only loads after you accept cookies in our banner, it does not run before that. Once active, it may collect information such as your IP address, browser and device details, pages visited, and actions taken on our site. This data is shared with Meta and may be used to show you more relevant ads on Facebook and Instagram.

For a full breakdown of the cookies and tracking tools we use, and how to opt out, see our Cookie Policy.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc., via the Meta Pixel, for ad measurement and targeting. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

If you visit one of our three challenge sites (SMAC, WAC, or AAC), that site's own Privacy Policy governs any information you provide there, including names, emails, and payment details.

Your Rights

You can limit Meta Pixel tracking using your browser settings or Meta's ad preferences, as described in our Cookie Policy. For questions about this policy, reach out via natetempleton.com.

Children's Privacy

TAC Co.'s challenges are intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via natetempleton.com.
