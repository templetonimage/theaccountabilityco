The problem

You're Not Busy. You're Manual.

Most solopreneurs lose hours a week to the same small tasks, done the same slow way, every single time.

Copy, paste, repeat

Moving the same info from inbox to spreadsheet to calendar. Every lead, every client, every week.

"I'll automate it someday"

You've saved the tutorials and bookmarked the tools. Someday never shows up on the calendar.

Tools without systems

You pay for the apps. Nothing connects them, so you're still the glue holding it all together.

Follow-ups that slip

Invoices, reminders, and replies live in your head. That's where they get forgotten.

Proof standards

What Counts as a Build

One build per week. It has to be something you created that week, and you have to show it.

Counts

  • A new Make, Zapier, or n8n scenario that runs on its own
  • A new email automation or sequence with a trigger
  • A custom GPT, Claude project, or chatbot you configured for a real task
  • A script, shortcut, or spreadsheet formula that replaces manual work
  • A new documented workflow or SOP with at least one automated step

Doesn't count

  • Using a tool or bot you already had
  • Asking ChatGPT or Claude a question
  • Signing up for an app without setting anything up
  • Last month's build, resubmitted
  • A plan to build something next week

Proof = a screenshot or short screen recording of the build + 1 to 2 lines on what it replaces
